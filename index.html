The BeLive Difference

Smart Living,
Protected by BeLive.

More than just a room — every BeLive space is designed with smart security, privacy, and peace of mind in mind. From the moment you step into the building until you enter your own room, BeLive's IoT-powered security system helps you feel safer, more comfortable, and more in control.

  • Dual Smart Lock Protection — Every unit comes with 2 IoT smart locks: one at the main entrance and one for your own bedroom, giving you an extra layer of personal privacy and security.
  • Private Access Control — Unique PIN access, temporary guest codes, and app-controlled entry let you manage access easily without worrying about lost keys or access cards.
  • 24/7 CCTV Monitoring — CCTV coverage at the shared living area helps create a safer and more secure co-living environment for all tenants.
  • Smart App Integration — Lock, unlock, and track entry activity directly from your phone, anytime, anywhere.
  • Auto-Lock Technology — Doors automatically secure themselves, with battery alerts sent early to ensure uninterrupted protection.

Smart technology isn't just about convenience — it's about creating a safer, worry-free co-living experience where tenants can truly feel at home.
